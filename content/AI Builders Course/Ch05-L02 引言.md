## 原文

Lesson 38 of 68
引言 / Introduction

前面三个模块帮你建立了构建者心态，但这仅仅是一段漫长、激动人心却又充满波折的旅程的开端。




学习 GenAI 就像第一次学习使用计算机——它是一个持续且迭代的过程。你会在使用中不断进步，但要让学习之旅更高效，就必须保持在正确的航道上。这需要你对 GenAI 有第一性原理层面的理解。否则，你会不断被标题党吸引，把时间浪费在那些很快就会过时的东西上（比如向量数据库、某些特定的 RAG 小技巧、对二流模型做微调，等等）。构建者心态是载具，而本模块试图为你绘制一张地图。

English Original

The last three modules prepared you for the builder mindset, but it’s only the beginning of a long, exciting, but turbulent journey.




Learning GenAI is similar to learning computers for the first time – it’s continuous and iterative. You get better by using it, but you need to stay on the right course to be efficient in your learning journey. This requires a first principle understanding of GenAI. Otherwise, you will constantly get distracted by clickbait titles, and waste time on things that will become irrelevant (vector database, some specific RAG tricks, fine tuning subpar models, and so on). The builder mindset is a vehicle, and this module tries to draw you a map.
