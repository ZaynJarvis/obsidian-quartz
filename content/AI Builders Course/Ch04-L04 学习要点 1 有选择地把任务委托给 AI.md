# 学习要点 1：有选择地把任务委托给 AI

## 我的理解

上下文切换对人类认知成本极高，而现代技术（邮件、即时消息、社交媒体）即便在没有推送通知时，也会让人在潜意识中反复挂念，从而打断深度思考。Gmail Prioritizer 的价值不在于“可以用 AI”，而在于它针对性地解决了 TPM 的具体痛点：让 AI 代为监控收件箱，只在重要邮件出现时才打断工作流。这揭示了有选择地委托的核心原则：委托的出发点是消除瓶颈、保护专注力，而非炫技或追求自动化本身——“从需求出发”永远先于“能否用 AI 实现”。

学习要点 1：有选择地把任务委托给 AI / Learning 1: Delegate to AI selectively

我们之所以选择 Gmail Prioritizer 作为示例，不仅因为它能展示多项良好实践，更因为它是优化工作流的一种有效方式。能够心无旁骛地思考与工作对生产力至关重要，因为上下文切换对人类来说成本极高。遗憾的是，几乎所有现代技术都在分散我们的注意力。电子邮件、即时消息、电话、TikTok——它们带来便利，却也在不断打断我们的工作节奏。




即便没有主动推送通知，这些技术依然会让我们分心。工作时，我们可能会担心：“我是不是漏掉了什么？”我们害怕错过重要消息，比如经理发来的 Slack 消息，否则会让自己显得不够尽责。这促使我们时不时在脑海中惦记着去查看这些平台，从而造成上下文切换［脚注 1，请见页面底部的脚注］。这种无意识的注意力分散，会严重限制我们思考的深度。




通过引入 Gmail Prioritizer，我们可以减少这些干扰，从而保持专注与高效。




一个提升生产力的实用技巧，是把无关的念头交给其他工具去处理，让自己的大脑得以解放。例如，与其反复检查 SQL 查询是否跑完，不如设一个半小时的计时器，等响了再去看。同样地，与其时刻挂念着 Slack 消息或邮件，不如让一个 AI 来监控你的收件箱，只在出现重要或紧急内容时才通知你。这样，你就能在更长时间内保持专注，而不必担心错过关键信息。等到一天结束时，再统一处理所有非紧急的消息。




这一思路也解释了我们为什么选择用 AI 来对 Gmail 进行优先级分类。这不仅仅是因为“可以这样做”，更是因为它切实回应了一位虚构 TPM 的具体工作流与痛点。构建过程应当始终从识别需求与挑战出发，AI 只是众多可选方案之一，并不一定是最终答案。

English Original

Here we chose Gmail Prioritizer as the example not only because it showcases different good practices, but also because it's an effective approach for workflow optimization. Our ability to think and work without distractions is crucial for productivity, as context switching is very costly for humans. Unfortunately, nearly all modern technologies distract us. Emails, instant messages, phone calls, TikTok—they offer convenience but also constantly disrupt our workflow.




Even without active notifications, these technologies distract us. While working, we might worry, "Did I miss anything?" We fear missing important messages, like a Slack message from our manager, which could make us look bad. This compels us to mentally check these platforms periodically, causing context switching [footnote 1, check the end of the page for footnotes]. This unconscious attention split severely limits the depth of our thinking.




By implementing a Gmail Prioritizer, we can reduce these distractions, allowing us to maintain focus and productivity.




A practical trick to boost productivity is to offload unrelated thoughts to other tools, freeing your mind. For example, instead of repeatedly checking if a SQL query has finished running, set a timer for half an hour and check only when it rings. Similarly, instead of constantly thinking about Slack messages or emails, set up an AI to monitor your inbox and notify you only when something important or urgent arrives. This way, you can maintain longer periods of focus without worrying about missing critical messages. At the end of the day, you can process all the non-urgent messages together.




This approach highlights why we chose to use AI for Gmail prioritization. It's not just because we can do it, but because it addresses specific workflow and pain points of an imaginary TPM. The building process should always start from identifying needs and challenges, with AI as a potential option but not necessarily the ultimate solution.
